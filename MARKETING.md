## 📣 Marketing Channels

### 1. Google Ads
- **Residential**: "compare gas electricity prices", "cheap energy supplier UK", "switch gas electricity"
- **Commercial**: "business energy comparison", "UK business gas prices"
- Budget: £500/month起

### 2. Facebook/Instagram
- 目标: 英国家庭用户
- 广告: "How much could you save on energy?"

### 3. LinkedIn (Commercial)
- 联系中小企业主
- 目标: 1000 connections

### 4. Email营销
- 从 Companies House 获取企业邮箱
- 目标: 5000 潜在客户
- Residential: 从选举register获取地址

### 5. 内容营销
- 博客: "如何降低家庭/商业能源成本"
- SEO: energy-saving tips for UK homes/businesses
