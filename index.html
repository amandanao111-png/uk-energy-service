❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the energy calculator work?

Our calculator uses the latest Ofgem price caps and market data to estimate your annual energy costs. Enter your annual kWh usage and we'll show your current cost vs potential savings.

How much can I save by switching?

Most UK households save £250-£500 per year by switching energy suppliers. Businesses can save 15-25% on their energy bills.

Is switching energy difficult?

No! We handle all the paperwork. Simply enter your details, and we'll compare prices from all major suppliers and guide you through the switch.

Will my supply be interrupted?

No. Your gas and electricity supply will continue without interruption. The switch typically takes 2-3 weeks and is completely seamless.

What information do I need to switch?

You'll need your postcode, current supplier name, and recent bill showing your annual kWh usage for gas and electricity.
